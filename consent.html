1
Who we are
Rooted is a product of Rooted Technology Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales (company number 17273832). Rooted is currently in pre-launch testing ahead of a public release. Rooted is not affiliated with any regulated financial institution.
2
Age requirement
Rooted is intended for users aged 18 and over. By using the app, you confirm that you are at least 18 years old.
3
What this is
You're using a real, working version of the Rooted app with a small group of early testers, ahead of its public App Store launch. This is not a simulation — your account, your credits ("g"), and your activity are real. We intend for your account and activity to carry forward as the app develops, though in rare cases (such as a major technical migration) some data may need to be reset; if this ever happens, we will notify you directly beforehand.
4
Not a financial product (FCA disclaimer)
Rooted is not a bank or a regulated financial institution, and this app is not regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority (FCA) of the United Kingdom. Nothing in the app constitutes financial advice, a financial promotion, or an offer of regulated payment services. Rooted does not link to your bank account or card at this stage, and never asks for your banking credentials.
5
Tree planting — where things stand
Rooted converts everyday habits into credits intended to fund real tree planting through ecological partners. We are actively working to confirm a live planting partner; until that partnership is operational, credits accumulate in your account but have not yet resulted in a physical tree being planted on your behalf. We'll update this document and notify users directly once real planting fulfilment begins.
6
Seed and demo accounts
A small number of researcher-managed profiles exist in the app to help demonstrate social features like Friends and Grove to new users. These are clearly not real people, and are disclosed here for transparency.
7
Your data (UK GDPR)
We collect:
  • Your name, email address, and in-app activity (credits earned, friends, groups, trees added to your account)
  • If you grant permission, your daily step count from Apple Health — used only to check whether you've reached a daily walking threshold. The actual step number is never stored, only whether you met the threshold that day. This data is never used for advertising and is never sold. You can revoke this permission at any time in your iOS Settings without losing access to the rest of the app.
We process your data on the basis of your consent. You can withdraw this consent at any time by requesting deletion of your account, as described in Section 8.

We retain your data for as long as your account remains active, and delete it within 30 days of a valid deletion request.

We do not collect financial or card data at this stage. Your data is confidential, is never sold, and is not shared with third parties beyond what's needed to operate the app (e.g. our database provider, Supabase, hosted in London). You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us below.
8
Your rights
You can stop using Rooted and request deletion of your account and data at any time, without needing a reason. This does not affect data already processed prior to your request. Under UK GDPR, you also have the right to access or correct your data.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).
