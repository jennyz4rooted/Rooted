Who we are
Rooted is a product of Rooted Technology Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales (company number 17273832). Rooted is currently in pre-launch testing ahead of a public release.
2
Age requirement
Rooted is intended for users aged 18 and over. By using the app, you confirm that you are at least 18 years old.
3
What this is
You are using a real, working version of the Rooted app with a small group of early testers, ahead of its public App Store launch. It is delivered through Apple's TestFlight, which means it is a beta version. It may have bugs, may crash, and features may change or stop working, so please use it at your own risk and do not rely on it for anything important.

Your account, your credits ("g") and your activity are real, and we intend for them to carry forward as the app develops. In rare cases (such as a major technical migration or a beta build expiring) some data may need to be reset. If that happens, we will tell you beforehand where we can.

You are welcome to share the TestFlight link and tell friends about Rooted. Your use of TestFlight is also covered by Apple's own TestFlight Terms of Service.
4
Not a financial product
Rooted is not a bank or a regulated financial institution, and this app is not regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority (FCA) of the United Kingdom. Nothing in the app constitutes financial advice, a financial promotion, or an offer of regulated payment services. Rooted does not ask for your online banking username or password.
5
Tree planting and where things stand
Rooted converts everyday habits into credits intended to fund real tree planting through ecological partners. We are actively working to confirm a live planting partner. Until that partnership is operational, credits accumulate in your account but have not yet resulted in a physical tree being planted on your behalf. We will update this document and notify users directly once real planting fulfilment begins.
6
Seed and demo accounts
A small number of researcher-managed profiles exist in the app to help demonstrate social features like Friends and Grove to new users. These are clearly not real people, and are disclosed here for transparency.
7
Your choices
Required to use Rooted
Your name, email address and in-app activity (credits earned, friends, groups, trees added to your account). Without these we cannot run your account. If you do not want us to hold this data, you can delete your account at any time (see Section 9).
Automatic beta data (cannot be switched off)
While you use a TestFlight beta, Apple automatically collects crash logs and usage data and shares them with us. This is part of how TestFlight works, and it cannot be turned off. You can stop it by deleting the beta app from your device.
Optional: your choice, and off until you switch it on
  • Apple Health steps. Only if you allow it. You can turn it off at any time in iOS Settings, and the rest of the app keeps working.
  • Linking a card. Only if you tap "Link card". Otherwise nothing is linked. This is currently test mode only (see Section 8).
  • Feedback. Only if you choose to send it, for example through TestFlight.
8
Your data: what we collect, why, and who handles it
What we collect and why
  • Your name and email address, to create and secure your account and to contact you about the test.
  • Your in-app activity, to run the app, work out your credits and show you your progress.
  • Your daily step count from Apple Health, if you allow it. We use it only to check whether you have reached a daily walking threshold. The step number itself is never stored, only whether you met the threshold that day. This data is never used for advertising and is never sold.
  • Card linking, if you choose it. We store a record that a card is linked, and any test-mode transaction amounts. This feature is in test mode only, using our card-linking provider's sandbox, so no real card, money or purchase is involved. As Rooted develops, we intend to connect with card-linked offers and/or Open Banking providers, always with your explicit consent, to detect real purchases at participating merchants. We will update this document and tell testers directly before any real transaction data is collected.
  • Crash logs, usage data and feedback from TestFlight, which may include information about your device and any screenshots you attach. Please do not include other people's personal details in screenshots. We use this to fix problems and improve the app, and may contact you about it.
We also use activity data to understand how testers use the app. We may share anonymised, combined figures (such as total users and how many people return) with investors and partners. These figures do not identify you.

We process your data on the basis of your consent, and you can withdraw it at any time (see Section 9).
Who else handles your data
  • Supabase, our database and login provider. Your account data is stored primarily on servers in London, UK. Supabase and its own service providers may access it from outside the UK, under standard legal safeguards. Supabase handles it only on our instructions, under a data processing agreement.
  • Apple, through TestFlight and Apple Health. Apple handles TestFlight data under its own Privacy Policy. Your Apple Health data stays on your device, and we only receive what is described above.
  • Our card-linking provider, only if you choose to link a card.
We never sell your data, and we do not share it with anyone else except where the law requires.
9
Keeping and deleting your data, and your rights
We keep your data for as long as your account is active, and delete it within 30 days of a valid deletion request.
How to opt out
  • Stop step tracking: turn it off in iOS Settings. Nothing else changes.
  • Leave the test: delete the app from your device. This removes what is stored on your phone but does not delete your Rooted account. To delete your account and data, email developer@rootedtheapp.com.
  • Apple's data: Apple keeps its own TestFlight data under its own policy. To ask Apple to delete it, contact Apple directly.
You can withdraw your consent at any time, without giving a reason. This does not affect anything we did before you withdrew. If you withdraw consent for your name, email and activity, we can no longer run your account, so we will delete it.

Under UK GDPR you also have the right to ask for a copy of your data, to have it corrected or deleted, and to object to or restrict how we use it. If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data, you can complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).
